Integrity in Every Harvest

Kiger Logging (pronounced like the Kiger Mustangs) is a family-owned forestry business based in Lewisburg, PA. Owner-operator Ken Kiger brings 45 years of hands-on experience in selective harvesting and woodland management.

We prioritize integrity, responsible harvesting, and building lasting relationships with landowners. By using low-impact equipment, we protect your forest floor and ensure the long-term health of your woodlands.

Silviculture Experts

We practice Silviculture—the science of controlling forest growth and health to meet the needs of landowners and wildlife. This selective approach prevents rot in unmanaged forests and promotes sustainable regrowth.
