The Science of Forestry

The Art of Silviculture

At Kiger Logging, we practice Silviculture—the art and science of controlling the growth, health, and quality of forests [5, 8].

Unlike clear-cutting, selective harvesting targets mature or at-risk trees. This approach prevents rot in unmanaged forests, protects the forest floor with low-impact equipment, and promotes sustainable regrowth [4, 8, 9].
