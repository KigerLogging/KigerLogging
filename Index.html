Built on Integrity and Experience

Kiger Logging (pronounced like the Kiger Mustangs) is a family-owned and operated forestry business based in Lewisburg, PA [1, 2]. Owner-operator Kenneth Kiger was born and raised in Pennsylvania and brings over four decades of hands-on experience to every job [1, 3].

We have sustained our company by building trust with local landowners; in fact, 70% of our business comes from word-of-mouth referrals from satisfied neighbors [2, 4].

Selective Harvesting & Silviculture

We practice Silviculture—the art and science of controlling forest growth and health to meet the needs of landowners and wildlife [8, 9].

What We Buy

We are actively seeking to purchase standing hardwood timber on tracts of 5 acres or more in the Union County area and surrounding Central PA (30+ mile radius) [7, 10, 11].

  • Red Oak & White Oak [1, 7]
  • Walnut [1, 7]

Landowner Benefits

Selective harvesting prevents unmanaged forests from developing rot and becoming unsafe [12, 14]. Our approach includes:

  • Low-impact yarding to protect soil [2, 6]
  • Promoting healthy regrowth [6, 8]
  • Long-term forest value protection [1, 9]
